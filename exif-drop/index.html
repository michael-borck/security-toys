Every photograph is a file, and the file has opinions about you. Drop one in and read what it says besides the image.

Nothing you drop here leaves your device. There is no server, no upload, no storage and no analytics anywhere in this page — the file is read in your browser's memory and forgotten when you close the tab. You can prove it: disconnect from the network first, and everything below still works. Keep your own screen to yourself; do not put a photo of somewhere you spend time on a projector.

The single photo is the demonstration; the pattern is the danger. One image gives a place and a second. A handful of images from the same camera share a serial number, and their coordinates cluster around the few places a person actually spends time — home, work, the school gate. Nobody had to follow anyone. This is why forensic investigators care about metadata, and why platforms that strip it are doing you a favour you never asked for.
