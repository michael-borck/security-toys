“Just buy a better detector”

Drag the dial all the way along. There is nowhere good to stand. Three in ten thousand is a base rate of 0.03%, and a detector that is right nine times out of ten still drowns you in wrong answers, because it is wrong about a much larger pile. The stealthy intrusion is not beating your detector — it is beating your budget. That is why detection is never finished at the tool: it is finished at triage, correlation, and deciding what you have chosen not to look at.
